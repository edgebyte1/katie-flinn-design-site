About Katie

The story behind otherworldly treasures

Our Founder

Katie Flinn

Fine Jewellery Designer & Mentor

I create otherworldly treasures through the ancient art of lost wax casting. Each piece is handcrafted with sterling silver, solid gold, and carefully sourced gemstones — primarily Australian opals that capture light in impossible ways.

My journey began with a deep fascination for the intersection of fantasy and craftsmanship. I wanted to create jewellery that tells stories — pieces that feel like they've emerged from another realm, yet are grounded in exceptional quality and traditional technique.

Today, I work with clients worldwide on custom commissions, maintain an ever-evolving collection of ready-made pieces, and mentor the next generation of jewellery makers through structured online courses. My passion lies in preserving the integrity of handcraft while pushing the boundaries of what's possible in fine jewellery design.

Integrity

Every piece is made to last. I use only quality materials and timeless techniques that stand the test of time.

Creativity

I embrace the fantastical and push design boundaries, creating pieces that spark imagination and wonder.

Accessibility

Through courses and mentoring, I share the craft so others can create their own otherworldly treasures.

The Craft

Lost Wax Casting Process

01

Design & Carve

Each design is hand-carved in jeweller's wax, capturing every detail and texture.

02

Create Mould

The wax model is attached to a tree and encased in plaster to create a negative mould.

03

Burnout & Cast

The mould is kiln-fired to burn away the wax, then molten metal is poured into the cavity.

04

Finish & Polish

The raw casting is refined, stones are set, and the piece is polished to perfection.
