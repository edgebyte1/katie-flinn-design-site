The Maker

Hey, I'm Katie Flinn

"My work is intuitive, expressive and guided by the natural flow of creativity."

What started in a tiny blue room in Pushkar, India in 2016 has become a full creative universe. I handcraft every piece from start to finish — currently from my van while travelling Australia.

I'm inspired by fantasy worlds, organic textures and undulating, melting movement. Each piece flows seamlessly, as if it was found out in the world already made.
