Our Services

Shop Jewellery

Katie's ready-to-wear collection features a curated selection of unique handcrafted pieces. Each design is created individually using the traditional lost wax casting process, ensuring that no two pieces are exactly alike.

Whether you're looking for statement rings, delicate earrings, or bold necklaces, you'll find pieces that combine elegance with the artistry of traditional metalsmithing. Every item in the shop has been thoughtfully designed and executed to the highest standards of craftsmanship.

Custom Commissions

Have an idea for a bespoke piece? Katie specializes in bringing your vision to life. The custom commission process begins with a consultation where you can discuss your ideas, inspirations, and preferences.

From there, Katie will develop sketches and concepts, refining the design until it perfectly captures your vision. Once approved, she'll cast the piece using the lost wax casting technique, ensuring exceptional quality and detail. Custom commissions are perfect for engagement rings, heirlooms, special occasion pieces, or any design you've been dreaming of.

Each piece tells a story and demonstrates the versatility and quality of Katie's work. While these specific pieces are no longer available, they serve as excellent references if you'd like to commission something similar or draw inspiration for your own unique design.
