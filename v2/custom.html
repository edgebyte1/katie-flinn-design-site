Our Custom Design Process

Understanding lost wax casting and how we bring your vision to life through traditional metalworking techniques

Step 1

Consultation

We begin with a detailed consultation to understand your vision, preferences, and inspirations. This is where we explore your design direction and discuss any specific requirements or constraints.

Step 2

Design Development

Katie creates sketches and develops the design based on your feedback. We refine details, proportions, and specifications until the design perfectly captures your vision.

Step 3

Lost Wax Casting

Using the ancient lost wax casting technique, Katie sculpts a wax model that becomes the prototype. The model is invested in a ceramic mold and cast in your chosen metal—a process perfected over centuries.

Step 4

Finishing & Setting

The cast piece is carefully refined, polished, and finished. Any gemstones are professionally set, and final adjustments ensure your piece is perfect.

Step 5

Quality Check

Every piece undergoes rigorous quality inspection. We verify dimensions, finish quality, and stone security before delivery to ensure your piece meets our exacting standards.

Step 6

Delivery

Your finished piece is carefully packaged and delivered to you. We provide care instructions and lifetime support for your custom creation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the timeline for a custom commission? +

Most custom commissions take 6-8 weeks from design approval to delivery. Complex pieces or pieces with multiple revisions may take longer. We'll confirm the exact timeline during your consultation and keep you updated throughout the process.

Can you work with my existing jewellery? +

Absolutely. We can incorporate gemstones from existing pieces, repurpose metals, or use your jewellery as inspiration. Let us know during your initial consultation if you'd like to include existing pieces in your custom design.

What metals do you work with? +

We work with a variety of precious metals including 14K and 18K gold (yellow, white, and rose), sterling silver, and platinum. Each metal has different working properties and aesthetic qualities, and Katie can advise on the best choice for your design.

How much does a custom commission cost? +

Custom commissions range from £800 for simple designs to £2,500+ for complex bespoke pieces. The final price depends on design complexity, materials chosen, and gemstones. We provide an accurate quote after reviewing your design requirements.

What happens if I don't like the design sketch? +

Revisions are an important part of the design process. Depending on your commission package, you'll have opportunities to request changes. We'll work with you until the design is exactly what you envision before proceeding to production.

Do you offer international shipping? +

Yes, we ship internationally. Shipping costs and delivery times vary depending on your location. We'll provide shipping details and options once your piece is ready for dispatch.
